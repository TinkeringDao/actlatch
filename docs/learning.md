# German guided play

The sample catalog provides four original German activity templates for
caregiver-selected preschool guided play. It is an engineering
fixture, not a validated curriculum or a developmental assessment. Every
activity ships with `parent_reviewed: false`.

## Review before child use

An actual child-facing session must deny an unreviewed activity. An explicit
simulation may demonstrate these synthetic templates without treating them as
approved. Simulation must not activate a microphone, camera, speaker, robot,
model, or external request merely to show the catalog.

A caregiver reviews the exact prompts, story themes, concepts, language and
physical materials before marking content reviewed. Editing reviewed content
requires fresh review and resetting its review flag. The flag records an
authoring decision; it does not authenticate a parent or prove hardware,
privacy, speech quality, or runtime readiness. Keep approval controls outside
the child's voice interface.

After review, harmless repeat play within the approved activity envelope
does not need approval on every turn. New permissions, materially changed
content, or new learning goals require caregiver review.

## Use with a caregiver

Offer a choice, ask one short question, and accept listening, pointing,
showing, speaking, or stopping. A caregiver selects safe large objects and
remains responsible for supervision. This system cannot babysit, locate a
missing child, monitor health, diagnose emotions, or establish that a child
is safe.

`story_turns` invites imagination; `color_sort` invites categorization;
`count_objects` explores quantities; and `drawing_chat` invites the child's
own explanation. Drawing chat works without a camera. No camera recognition
should overrule what the child says their picture means.

Treat recognition uncertainty as a system issue. Ask for clarification or
offer another way to answer; do not turn a misheard response into a wrong
answer or a lower ability estimate. Avoid fixed age milestones, intelligence
scores, comparisons with other children, and manipulative rewards. A useful
record describes a particular activity and support used, rather than claiming
that a concept has been mastered.

Child-facing speech stays German. Use locally approved repair phrases when
generation fails. If a local service is unavailable, pause or use an approved
cached activity; do not silently send child data to a hosted fallback.
Learning or conversational adaptation cannot change software, permissions,
retention, or the approved content boundary.

## Research context

Guided play combines choice with purposeful support; it is broader than
spelling drills. This follows the approach described in
[NAEYC's development and learning guidance](https://www.naeyc.org/node/3796).
German early-language guidance also emphasizes dialogue, everyday activity,
and relationships with caregivers, as described by
[Frühe Chancen](https://www.fruehe-chancen.de/en/informationen-fuer-fachkraefte/uebergreifende-themen-im-kita-alltag/sprachliche-bildung/was-ist-alltagsintegrierte-sprachliche-bildung).

A controlled vocabulary study found no overall advantage of robot-assisted
conditions over its tablet-only condition. Its procedure is not evidence of
this project's educational effectiveness:
[van den Berghe et al., 2021](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2021.676248/full).
Child speech recognition also needs evaluation on the actual language and
audio path; an older English result cannot establish German performance:
[Janssens et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.17394v2).

The proposed privacy and relationship boundaries draw on
[UNICEF's guidance on AI and children](https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/reports/policy-guidance-ai-children).
They are design requirements, not a certification or evidence that the
commissioning checks have passed.
